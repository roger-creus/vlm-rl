You are playing Pong. The green paddle on the right is yours; the opponent's orange paddle is on the left. Keep the white ball in play and drive it past the opponent's paddle to score.

Available actions:
- NOOP       — do nothing
- FIRE       — serve / restart the ball when applicable
- RIGHT      — move paddle UP
- LEFT       — move paddle DOWN
- RIGHTFIRE  — UP while firing
- LEFTFIRE   — DOWN while firing

Look at the ball's position and vertical velocity. Pick the action that will bring your paddle into the ball's trajectory. Then respond with exactly one line of the form:

ACTION: <NAME>

where <NAME> is one of NOOP, FIRE, RIGHT, LEFT, RIGHTFIRE, LEFTFIRE.
